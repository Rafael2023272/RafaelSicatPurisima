About Me

A page from the diary...

❦ ❦ ❦

~ 12 07, 25 ~

"Technology is the modern canvas, and I intend to fill every inch of it."

I'm a Creative Computing Student from Bath Spa University RAK who sees technology as a modern creative canvas. My work sits at the intersection of design and development, where visual ideas meet technical execution.

✦

I enjoy building digital experiences that are both functional and engaging — from pixel-perfect interfaces and full-stack web applications to interactive 2D games and photography projects. Each project is an opportunity for me to combine creativity with problem-solving.

Based in Sharjah, UAE, my goal is to create technology that feels seamless and intuitive, allowing the experience itself to take center stage.
